  content: |
    GHL Bangladesh began with conversations between <a href="https://www.facebook.com/vidmshariful" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" aria-label="Shariful Islam on Facebook (opens in a new tab)">Shariful Islam</a> and me. We kept talking about how many people in Bangladesh work with GoHighLevel every day, yet hardly ever meet in person. We wanted a chance to sit together, compare experiences, and get to know the people doing the same work.

    So we decided to organise an unofficial, community-driven meetup. The idea was simple: GHL users in one room, asking questions and sharing what was actually working for them.

    Shariful and I organised that gathering together. For me, starting GHL Bangladesh was a way to make those conversations happen: people here meeting face to face, sharing practical knowledge, and learning from one another.
